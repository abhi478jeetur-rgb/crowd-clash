# Crowd Clash — style

Neon night stadium. Deep navy background (#0b1026 → #131a3a vertical gradient) with a darker
indigo track (#1d2650) and glowing cyan edge rails (#22d3ee). The crowd is drawn as simple
rounded runner glyphs (circle head, rounded body, running bob) with varied shirt hues and a
large white count pill above the blob. Gates are translucent half-track panels with bright
borders, colour-coded by operation: + green (#22c55e), × violet (#a855f7), − red (#ef4444),
÷ amber (#f59e0b), always with big bold operation text. Traps are dark strips with red hazard
chevrons and spikes; bonus zones are golden drums (#fbbf24). The enemy crowd is red (#f87171).
Typography: system-ui, heavy weights for numbers; HUD is a single centred line. Juice: pop
numbers on every resolution, particles on gains, screen shake + hit-stop on traps and clash
hits, slow drum-synced clash. All art is procedural canvas — no external assets, no webfonts.
Audio is synthesized WebAudio only: pitch-rising gate blips by combo, trap thud, bonus
arpeggio, clash drums, win/loss jingles, and a light bass+hat BGM sequencer while playing.
